Privacy Policy

This policy explains how CrazyAlarm handles your data and how you can control, withdraw, or delete it.

Scope and Core Principles

This policy applies to the CrazyAlarm iOS app (“the app”). The app is provided by the CrazyAlarm developer. It does not create a separate user account, and its core alarm features can be used without signing in.

We follow the principles of necessity, data minimization, and user control. We do not sell personal data, serve advertising based on personal data, or track you across apps or websites.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app stores the data needed to provide alarms, timing, sleep sounds, statistics, and similar features on your device. If you enable sync, the data covered by sync is stored in your private iCloud database; the developer does not receive it through this sync feature. If you submit feedback, the developer receives only the feedback you submit and the diagnostic information listed below.

  • Alarm times, names, repeat rules, sounds, snooze settings, and dismissal mission settings.
  • Alarm activity and mission completion history, statistics, timer state, preferences, and favorites.
  • Reference images for photo or barcode missions. They are kept in an app-specific directory, excluded from iCloud device backups, and are not synced with alarm settings. Images captured while completing a mission are used only on the device for recognition or comparison.
  • Acceleration, device motion, and step counts needed for movement missions, plus microphone volume needed for the blowing mission. Sensor data is used only for immediate evaluation and is not uploaded. The blowing mission does not save audio recordings.

Optional iCloud Sync

iCloud sync is off by default. When you enable it, alarm settings, your nickname, custom avatar, and historical alarm, snooze, habit, and mission results sync through your private iCloud database across devices signed in to the same Apple Account. Statistics are calculated from history; the same planned reminder is counted once, using the earliest completed device record. The preset target code value, code type, and display name for scan missions sync as part of alarm mission settings. Active alarm state, mission reference images, live camera images, and prompts and answers during mission execution are not uploaded through this sync feature. Device backups are controlled separately in system settings. The calendar region follows each device’s system region and is not overridden by a region synced from another device.

You can turn off sync at any time; this does not automatically delete existing cloud data. Under Profile & iCloud, you can delete cloud alarm settings, execution history, your nickname, and avatar. Deletion turns off sync on this device and keeps its local data. Re-enabling sync may upload local data again; other devices with sync still on may also upload data again. To prevent this, first turn off sync on all devices. iCloud data is also subject to the terms for your Apple Account and iCloud. In mainland China, iCloud is operated by GCBD.

Data Submitted with Feedback

When you choose to send feedback, it is stored in CrazyAlarm’s public CloudKit database. Database access is controlled by CloudKit permissions, and other regular users cannot view your feedback through the app.

  • Required information: feedback type, feedback text, and submission time.
  • Diagnostic information: app version and build number, iOS version, device type, and system region, used to investigate compatibility issues.
  • Optional information: email address; users in mainland China may also provide a phone number. Contact details are used only to reply to that feedback.
  • Feedback is kept until the issue has been handled or contact is no longer needed. You can request access or deletion through Send Feedback. Relevant records may be retained for the necessary period when required by law, security audits, or dispute resolution.

Location and Weather

If you allow location access, the app requests your current location once, when needed after an alarm mission is completed, to show local weather using Apple location, geocoding, and WeatherKit services. Location is obtained only while this feature is used. Weather results are cached in memory for about 30 minutes, and the app does not build a long-term location history.

Denying location access does not affect core features such as alarms, timing, or dismissal missions; only the weather card becomes unavailable. Apple’s privacy policy applies to its processing of related requests.

System Permissions and Your Choices

Depending on the features you choose, the app may request access to notifications, the camera, microphone, Motion & Fitness, location, or the photo picker. Permissions are not used in situations unrelated to those features.

  • You can revoke system permissions at any time in iOS Settings. The corresponding missions or weather feature may then become unavailable.
  • The photo picker accesses only the image you select when choosing an avatar; it does not browse your entire photo library.
  • You can turn off iCloud sync and continue using alarms on this device. You can also submit feedback without providing contact details.

Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

The app does not sell, rent, or provide your data to advertisers or data brokers. To deliver features you choose to use, data may be processed by Apple services including CloudKit, iCloud, location, geocoding, WeatherKit, notifications, and the App Store.

We do not disclose relevant data to other third parties unless you instruct us to do so, disclosure is necessary to provide the features above, or it is lawfully required by legislation, courts, or regulatory authorities.

Storage, Security, and Deletion

On-device data is kept in the iOS app sandbox, and mission reference images use system file protection. Cloud data is stored by Apple’s CloudKit infrastructure. No network or storage method can guarantee absolute security, but we use reasonable measures within the app’s scope to reduce unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

  • When you delete an alarm or mission, its settings and any reference images no longer in use are also removed.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes on-device data in its sandbox. Data previously synced to iCloud is not automatically deleted.
  • Under Profile & iCloud, you can delete cloud alarm settings, execution history, your nickname, and avatar, and turn off sync.
  • For feedback or other data you cannot delete yourself, contact us through Send Feedback.

Children and Minors

The app is not primarily intended for children or minors and does not advertise or create profiles based on minor status. Users below the age at which they may legally consent independently where they live should use features that require a network connection or content submission only after a guardian has read and agreed to this policy.

Policy Updates and Contact

We may update this policy when features, data-processing practices, or legal requirements change. We will indicate material changes through an in-app notice or release information where possible and show the latest date on this page.

To access, correct, export, or delete relevant data, withdraw consent, or ask about this policy, contact us through Settings > Send Feedback. To protect data security, we may need to verify information necessary for your request.
